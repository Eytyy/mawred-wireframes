## 2. Content inventory — as it exists today

### TAB 1 — About the Program

1. **Heading + intro** — "What is Stand for Art?" Supports Arab-region artists/cultural actors facing risk to safety/security, whether from their artistic output/activities or from an unsafe environment (in-country or abroad). Launched 2016 after a study of regional risks and available support.
2. **Prioritized risk types** — kidnapping/detention, imprisonment, threats of physical harm, violence and abuse, prosecution — whether by legal or illegal means.
3. **Program goals** — safeguard the right to free expression and to live/work in safety and dignity; provide **temporary** support toward that end.
4. **Who is this program for** — (a) those in danger due to their artistic/cultural expression or production; (b) those in danger due to an unsafe environment, in-country or abroad.
5. **Forms of support** — two distinct modes:
   - **Emergency fund** (monetary) covering one or more of: living/accommodation expenses (country of residence or host country) up to **6 months**; travel expenses (tickets, visa costs, internal transport); legal support (advice/lawyers); other expenditure-based support (e.g. psychological and/or physical health care).
   - **Referral** (non-monetary) — connecting beneficiaries to: short-term artistic residencies (Arab region or abroad) as a temporary safe haven; international orgs supporting at-risk artists / offering residencies (program backs the application with a recommendation letter); advocacy orgs or lawyers for free legal advice.
6. **Eligibility criteria** — must originate from an Arab country (regardless of ethnicity/citizenship, resident or abroad); must be an artist or cultural actor; must be in peril or face a tangible and verifiable threat.

### TAB 2 — Application Guidelines

1. **How to apply** — fill out the **encrypted** application form (JotForm link); Arabic-only except English-required fields; attach required documents (list below).
2. **Required documents** — (1) passport/ID/any identification document; (2) short bio or detailed CV; (3) previous works or links (up to 3); (4) **two recommendation letters** from individuals/orgs aware of the risk situation, with their contact info; (5) official invitation from a host entity (if available).
3. **Two privacy notes** — (Note 1) the encrypted form **cannot be saved and resumed** for technical/privacy reasons; (Note 2) all information is confidential and used internally by the Stand for Art team.
4. **The selection process** — 5 numbered steps: (1) eligibility/completeness/documents check; (2) team verifies accuracy of information, sometimes consulting trusted individuals/orgs bound to confidentiality; (3) submission to the **steering committee** (6 artists/cultural managers, gender-diverse and geographically distributed) which approves/rejects and sets the form of support based on case gravity; (4) team informs applicant of the decision and, if accepted, sends the proposed support; (5) agreement signed between Culture Resource and the beneficiary.

### TAB 3 — FAQ

1. **Six Q&A pairs, fully in English** (a real content difference from PA/Wijhat, whose EN FAQs are empty shells):
   - Journalists/photojournalists? — no, unless working in cultural/artistic journalism with published work in that field.
   - Artists not at risk? — no (redirects to other Culture Resource programs).
   - Organizations at risk of closure/bankruptcy? — no; individuals only, whether org-affiliated or independent (redirects to other programs).
   - Family members covered? — support goes to the artist/cultural actor only, but their family conditions and dependents are taken into consideration.
   - How much financial support? — **no preset figures**; amount set by severity/urgency, program budget, and cost of living in residence/host country.
   - Help obtaining entry visas? — cannot intervene in visa processes, but can provide a support-certifying letter if needed.

## 3. Observations & issues (Stand for Art specific)

- **Confidentiality reframes the entire apply flow.** The defining divergence. The form is **encrypted**, **can't be saved/resumed**, routes to **JotForm rather than the `apply.mawred.org` portal**, and carries explicit data-confidentiality notes. Confidentiality assurances read as first-class content here, not fine print.
- **No dates, no rounds, no call status.** Rolling/continuous intake with no deadlines or results announcements.
- **Support is variable, not a fixed grant.** "How much" is explicitly criteria-driven with **no preset figure**, and support comes in two shapes (emergency fund _and/or_ referral, the latter non-monetary).
- **Referral as a support mode is unique so far.** Non-monetary support (residency connections, recommendation letters to third parties, advocacy/legal referrals) has no analogue in PA or Wijhat.
- **FAQ is actually populated (in English).** First program audited where the EN FAQ has real content — six Q&As. The _gap_ on other pages is a migration/content issue, not a page-design one.
- **Selection by steering committee, not a jury.** 6-member steering committee (vs. PA's per-discipline 3-member juries, Wijhat's per-round 3-member juries). Committee both approves/rejects **and** determines the form/level of support — a decision step the fixed-grant programs don't have.
- **Simplest contractual footprint of the three so far.** No installments/percentages, no signing-window days, no completion-window, no guarantors, no clawback clause, no citation/credit obligation stated. Just: committee decides support → applicant agrees → agreement signed.
- **Content-integrity / migration flags:**
  - (a) **Contact mismatch** — visible text `artist@mawred.org` (singular) vs. mailto `artists@mawred.org` (plural). Mirrors the Wijhat footer-mailto bug; reinforces the need for a site-wide contact-link QA pass.
  - (b) **No hero-summary block** — like PA (and unlike Wijhat), SFA has no above-tabs who/purpose/where/grant summary.
  - (c) **Instagram footer is an empty embed** (`-->` stray comment visible) — recurring global-footer artifact worth checking sitewide.
- **Sensitivity note for Phase 2.** This page is confidentiality-critical: nothing in the design should imply tracking, public listings, or non-encrypted submission.
- **Content unique to this program:** encrypted/non-resumable JotForm route, confidentiality notes, dual forms of support (emergency fund + referral), no-fixed-amount support model, steering-committee selection that sets support level, "rolling / always open" cadence with no dates.
