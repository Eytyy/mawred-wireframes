## 2. Content inventory — as it exists today

### TAB 1 — About the Program

1. **Heading + intro paragraph** — "What is the Production Awards Program?" Launched 2004; under-35 (birth-year range stated explicitly); Arab region incl. diaspora; lists the 5 disciplines.
2. **Scale line** — up to 25 projects/year, 5 per discipline; ~400 grants since 2004.
3. **Key dates** (3 sub-headings) — Open Call date · Deadline (date + time + Beirut timezone) · Results (month).
4. **Application form previews** — 5 downloadable PDF links, one per discipline (Visual Arts, Literature, Music & Sound Art, Performing Arts, Cinema).
5. **Required documents** — link to a PDF list.
6. **How to apply** — 4 numbered steps (register on portal → activation email → select program & fill form → save/return), each with a circle graphic. Plus a bold warning to read the guidelines first.
7. **Disciplines & grant amounts** — 5 disciplines, each: what it covers + max grant.
   - Performing Arts €8,500 · Visual Arts €8,500 · Literature €6,500 · Music & Sound Art €8,500 · Cinema €13,000
8. **Important notes / exclusions** — no reproductions of already-produced work; excludes websites, workshops, org support, academic/research projects, TV series.
9. **APPLY NOW button** (repeated CTA, links to portal).

### TAB 2 — Application Guidelines

1. **Who is eligible to apply** — birth-year range; Arab-origin regardless of residence; re-application rules for past recipients; who must submit per discipline (director / choreographer / writer / main musician / main artist or curator).
2. **Application terms** — Arabic-only (except English fields); file formats; no incomplete apps; one project per round; 18-month completion window; no equipment purchases; funds spent in/benefiting Arab region; under-18 extra documents.
3. **How are grantees selected** — jury per discipline (3 members, changes yearly); selection criteria (quality/relevance, originality, budget practicability, feasibility).
4. **Contractual procedures & obligations** — agreement issuance; IP-rights doc; installments (70% / 30%); 45-day signing window; extension/cancellation rules; clawback on cancellation; halt of final installment; one-time extension limits (6 mo / 1 yr cinema); **guarantor requirement** (two guarantors); **citation/credit obligation** (logo + support phrase in all outputs).
5. **APPLY NOW button** (repeated).

### TAB 3 — FAQs

1. **"Available only in Arabic"** — links to the Arabic page anchor. No English content.

### In-template footer

## 3. Observations & issues (Production Awards specific)

- **Prose-heavy where structure would help.** Disciplines/amounts (item 7), key dates (item 3), and how-to-apply (item 6) are all list-able / table-able data currently rendered as running text or loose headings.
- **CTA + apply flow is scattered.** "How to apply" (item 6), the APPLY NOW buttons (9, 14), form previews (4), and required docs (5) are all part of one logical "how to apply" story but are split across the About tab and repeated.
- **Dates live only in prose.** No at-a-glance status ("is the call open right now?"). A visitor must read to find out.
- **FAQs is an empty shell in English.** Consistent gap — recurs on other programs.
- **Two repeated data points that change each round:** grant range, and the open/deadline/results dates. Other programs also have these (in different shapes).
- **Content unique to this program:** per-discipline form previews, guarantor clause, citation/credit obligation, per-discipline jury structure, discipline-specific extension limits.
