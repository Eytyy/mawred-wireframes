## 2. Content inventory — as it exists today

### PRE-TAB — Header summary block (above the tabs)

Wijhat opens with a **four-part scannable summary above the tab strip**, each with its own heading and bullet list:

- **"Who is it for"** — individuals or groups; artists of all disciplines; cultural actors (independent or org-affiliated).
- **"For what purpose?"** — participate in: fairs, residencies, forums/conferences/seminars, workshops promoting creative/artistic production, training/capacity-building workshops.
- **"From where to where"** — the 3 permitted travel directions (Arab→Arab; outside→Arab; Arab→outside).
- **"The grant"** — up to €7,000 to cover: travel tickets, visa costs, accommodation, partial living expenses, internal transportation.
- **Arrows graphic** (`MawredWijhatArrows.png`).

> Note: this summary block duplicates content that also appears inside the tabs (grant amount, who's eligible, purpose). It's a marketing/at-a-glance layer.

### TAB 1 — About the Program

1. **Heading + intro** — "What is Wijhat?" Problem framing (travel funding, visa difficulty, networking gaps for Arab artists) → the program as response; up to €7,000; supports travel within/outside the Arab region.
2. **Scale line** — 25 to 30 grants a year, in 3 rounds. Note that living-expense support is partial ("travel support rather," not full living costs).
3. **Recommendation letter note** — Culture Resource can provide a visa recommendation letter to the embassy, arranged after the contract is signed.
4. **Duration note** — no fixed travel/stay duration; covers only the expenses for the period of the stated activity/event.
5. **Program Schedule & Application Dates** — intro line (applications accepted year-round; fixed evaluation windows per round).
6. **The 3 rounds** (each its own sub-heading) — Round 1 Feb (deadline 16 Jan 16:00 Beirut · announced 28 Feb) · Round 2 Jun (deadline 29 May 16:00 · announced 1 Jul) · Round 3 Oct (deadline 17 Sep 16:00 · announced 31 Oct).
7. **Deadline rules** — no early announcement of grantees; screening starts immediately after deadline; late applications roll to the next round if eligible.
8. **"When to apply" logic** — compare intended travel date against two dates: travel must be **after** results announced; application must be **before** that round's deadline.
9. **How are grantees selected** — screening for eligibility/completeness → 3-member independent jury, changes each round; jury = cultural actors/artists with Arab + international expertise; criteria center on intended destination and activity type.

### TAB 2 — Application Guidelines

1. **Who is eligible to apply** — must be from an Arab country (regardless of ethnicity/citizenship, resident or abroad); country of departure OR arrival must be in the Arab region; individuals or groups; artists of all disciplines + cultural actors (with examples); host may not apply on behalf of invitee (may only assist); under-18 allowed with guardian consent letter; re-application barred for 16 months / 4 rounds after a Wijhat grant; recipients of _other_ Mawred grants may still apply.
2. **How to apply as an INDIVIDUAL** — portal steps (register → activation email → log in → select "Wijhat" → individual form → save/return); Arabic-only except English fields; state travel purpose & fit with program aims; describe schedule/activities/expected outcomes; one application per round (single project, may have multiple destinations/dates); grantee responsible for visas (recommendation letter available post-signing).
3. **Documents — individual** — official invitation (or proof of acceptance in principle); passport photocopy; detailed travel budget (form comes with application); activity program if available; detailed CV; list of previous works (format/size rules: single PDF/Word/Zip ≤4 MB, or 4 JPEGs ≤1 MB each, or links for A/V). Incomplete apps discarded before jury.
4. **How to apply as a GROUP** — parallel portal steps (applicant applies on behalf of group → group form); Arabic-only except English fields; state purpose & fit _(line duplicated in source)_; one application per round; all group travel costs in a single itemized budget; visa/recommendation-letter note.
5. **Documents — group** — official invitation (or proof of acceptance for the group); photocopies of **all participants'** passports; detailed travel budget; activity program if available; list of previous works (same format/size rules). _(Note: group list omits the "detailed CV" line present in the individual list.)_ Incomplete apps discarded before jury.
6. **Contractual Procedures & Conditions** — contracts sent after results; installments **60–80% on signing / 20–40% on submission of financial + narrative reports**; 60-day signing window or grant canceled; actual-cost cap if travel costs less than estimated; grant used within **12 months** of announcement; citation/credit obligation (Wijhat + Culture Resource referenced in all publicity/media/reports); withhold 2nd installment if reports/obligations unmet; grant tied to the specific activity — annulled if travel doesn't happen, with a discretionary case-by-case repurposing option; clawback of unwarranted disbursed portion on cancellation. _(Source contains unresolved editorial comments in the clawback clause — flagged below.)_
7. **Contact line (in-tab)** — "For further questions, please contact the Wijhat team: wijhat@mawred.org"

## 3. Observations & issues (Wijhat specific)

- **Dual application tracks (individual vs. group).** The single biggest structural divergence from PA. Two near-parallel "how to apply" flows + two document lists sit back-to-back in the Guidelines tab, sharing ~80% of their content.
- **Rounds instead of a single annual call.** PA has one deadline/results pair; Wijhat has **three**, plus a "how to choose your round" decision aid (item 8), expressing a rolling-submission-with-fixed-windows model.
- **Above-the-tab summary block.** Wijhat front-loads a scannable who/purpose/where/grant summary. It duplicates in-tab content (grant amount, eligibility, purpose).
- **Editorial artifacts left in live copy.** Two content-integrity bugs: (a) the clawback clause contains visible unresolved editor comments ("((OR probably clearer… ))"); (b) the footer contact mailto links to `abbara@mawred.org` while the visible text says wijhat@. Suggests other pages may carry similar stray artifacts.
- **Duplicated line in group flow** — "Clearly express… purpose of the intended travel" appears twice (item 13). Confirms manual copy drift between the two tracks.
- **No disciplines/amounts matrix.** Unlike PA's 5-discipline grid, Wijhat is single-amount (up to €7,000, itemized by cost type — a cap + a covered-costs list rather than a per-discipline table).
- **Malformed headings from source** — several headings render with the question mark at the front ("?Who is it for", "?From where to where") — an RTL/punctuation artifact of the bilingual CMS.
- **FAQs is an empty shell in English.** Same gap as PA.
- **Content unique to this program:** individual-vs-group dual tracks, 3-round schedule + "when to apply" decision aid, "from where to where" travel-direction rule, visa recommendation-letter provision, arrows graphic.
