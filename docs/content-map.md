Where the content on each populated page came from, block by block. One section per page-type, appended as each is populated.

Records four things per block: the source it came from, what was shortened or rewritten, what was split out of a single live passage into more than one block, and what was left as a gap. Read it when a figure or a date on a wireframe needs checking, or when the next unit needs to know how the last one handled the same shape.

Copy is being rewritten, not migrated — everything below is wireframe-length adaptation for structure and volume, never final wording.

## Stand for Art

Sources: `content/programs/audit-stand-for-art.md` for substance; the live page (`mawred.org/stand-for-art/?lang=en`) for prose length. The audit and the live page agree line for line here — every figure, list and note in the audit is present on the page, including the six English FAQs — so nothing below is a gap the live page contradicts.

**The first programme whose English FAQ tab has real content.** Production Awards and Wijhat both carry only a pointer to the Arabic page; Stand for Art carries six Q&As in English, so C11 is populated for the first time and the "English FAQs don't exist" assertion stops being baked into the block.

**Confidentiality is the page's organising fact, not fine print.** The form is encrypted, routes to JotForm rather than `apply.mawred.org`, and can't be saved and reopened. That shapes three of the decisions below.
