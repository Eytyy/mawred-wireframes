Where the content on each populated page came from, block by block. One section per page-type, appended as each is populated.

Records four things per block: the source it came from, what was shortened or rewritten, what was split out of a single live passage into more than one block, and what was left as a gap. Read it when a figure or a date on a wireframe needs checking, or when the next unit needs to know how the last one handled the same shape.

Copy is being rewritten, not migrated — everything below is wireframe-length adaptation for structure and volume, never final wording.
